Beard trims & lineups

A beard either looks intentional or it doesn't. We shape, fade and edge yours until it clearly is.

Growing a beard is easy. Making it look deliberate, matched to your jaw, faded into your cut, edged clean at the cheeks and neck, is barber work. That's what you're paying for.

What a Slick Fades beard service includes

  • Shape consult: square, rounded, faded or full; we match the shape to your face, not a trend.
  • Cheek & neck lines: razor-finished, with hot lather. The line placement is what separates barbershop work from bathroom-mirror work.
  • Beard fade: blend the sideburns into the beard so cut and beard read as one look.
  • Conditioning finish: beard oil and a brush-out before you're up.

Make it the full reset

The move most guys make: haircut + beard together. One sitting and you walk out a different guy. Between full services, a quick lineup keeps the edges crisp for a wedding, an interview, or a weekend.

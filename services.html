The services

Every cut starts with a conversation and ends with a mirror check you'll actually enjoy. Here's everything we do at our North Olmsted shop.

  • Haircut & FadeSkin fade, taper fade, drop fade, burst fade, scissor cut, finished with a crisp lineup. About 35–45 minutes. See our fades page for the style breakdown.
  • Haircut + BeardThe full reset: your cut plus beard shaping, cheek fade and razor edges. About an hour.
  • Kids Cut (12 & under)Patient barbers who make first haircuts easy. Parents welcome chairside.
  • Beard Trim & LineupShape and symmetry, with the edges finished by razor instead of trimmers.
  • Hot Towel & Head ShavesStraight razor face shaves with hot towels, full head shaves, and razor cleanups between cuts.
  • Brows & WaxingEyebrow threading or wax, nose and ear wax, full face. A two-minute add-on to any cut.

Pricing is set in the chair by your barber and varies by length and style. Call (440) 328-6368 for today's prices.

What's included in every cut

Nobody gets rushed through the chair here. Every service at Slick Fades includes a consultation before the clippers turn on, hot lather on razor work, and a styling finish so you walk out the door camera-ready, not "fixed it at home" ready.

  • Consultation: bring a photo or describe it; we'll tell you straight what works for your head shape and hair type.
  • Precision fade work: skin, taper, drop, burst, mid or high. Fades are literally our name.
  • Razor lineup: hairline, temples and neck finished clean.
  • Styling & product: we finish the look and show you how to keep it.

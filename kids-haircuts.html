Kids haircuts

Google says we're 'good for kids', parents say it louder. Patient barbers, first-haircut friendly, and a giant bear on the wall to keep them entertained.

Bringing a kid to the barbershop shouldn't be a wrestling match. Our barbers cut kids' hair every week and know the difference between a nervous first-timer and a seven-year-old who already knows he wants a burst fade like his favorite player.

How we make it easy

  • Parents stay chairside: stand right next to the chair, hold a hand if needed.
  • First haircuts welcome: we go slow, take breaks, and yes, you can film it.
  • The mural helps: a giant airbrushed teddy bear with clippers is the best distraction in North Olmsted.
  • Real cuts, not kid cuts: fades, tapers, designs and classic styles, scaled to smaller heads.

Timing tips from our chairs

Weekday afternoons before 5pm are calmest for younger kids. Saturday mornings move fast, call (440) 328-6368 and we'll tell you the shortest wait.

Shaves & razor work

Hot towels, a fresh blade, and a barber who takes his time. The oldest service in the book, done the way it should be.

A real shave is slower than whatever happens in your bathroom, and that's the point. You lean back, the hot towel goes on, and fifteen minutes later your face looks like the razor was invented yesterday.

What we shave

  • Hot towel face shave: hot towel to open the skin, lather, straight razor pass, and a cold finish to close it up.
  • Head shave: the full dome, razor finished and even everywhere. Add a beard service and keep the whole look tight.
  • Razor cleanup: necklines, cheeks and edges between cuts, so the fade lasts longer than the week.

Who it's for

Guys before a wedding or an interview, guys who shave their head every week and are tired of doing the back blind, and anyone who's never had a hot towel shave and wants to see what the fuss is about. Pair it with a beard lineup or grab it on its own.

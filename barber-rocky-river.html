Barber near Rocky River, OH

Twelve minutes from the river, and your barber actually answers the phone. Imagine that.

If you're searching for a barber near Rocky River, you're about 12 min from one of the highest-rated chairs on Cleveland's west side. Slick Fades Barber Shop sits at 23601 Lorain Rd in North Olmsted, a quick run down Wooster Rd or I-90 to Lorain Rd.

Why Rocky River comes to Slick Fades

  • 4.9 stars across 113 Google reviews: earned one fade at a time.
  • Walk-ins welcome: no app, no waiting list. Come as you are.
  • Open late: till 7pm Tuesday through Saturday, and Sundays till 5pm, when many Rocky River barbershops are closed.
  • No guesswork: you talk the cut through with your barber before the clippers ever turn on.
  • Every style: skin fades, tapers, drop fades, scissor work, beard sculpting and kids cuts.

Getting here from Rocky River

Take Wooster Rd south to Center Ridge, catch Columbia south to Lorain Rd, or jump on I-90 west and cut down through Westlake. Either way you land on Lorain Rd at 23601. Easy parking right out front, and you can grab food or run errands at Great Northern Mall while you're on this side of town.

We're open Sundays 11–5, handy when the weekend sneaks up and Monday morning has meetings on it.

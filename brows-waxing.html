Brows & waxing

Clean brows change the whole cut. Threading or wax, your call, and we handle the nose and ears while we're at it.

You can get the sharpest fade in Ohio and it still won't read clean if the brows are doing their own thing. This is the two-minute detail work that makes the mirror check land.

What we offer

  • Eyebrow threading: precise, clean lines with no chemicals on the skin. The move if wax irritates you.
  • Eyebrow waxing: faster than threading and just as sharp. Your barber shapes to your face, not to a stencil.
  • Nose & ear wax: thirty seconds each and you stop thinking about it for a month.
  • Full face wax: cheeks and forehead cleaned up so the beard line does all the talking.

Add it to any cut

Most guys tack this onto a fade or a beard lineup without adding real time to the visit. Ask your barber when you sit down, or come in for brows alone. Walk-ins work for this too.

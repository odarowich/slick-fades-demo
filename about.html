More than a haircut

A community shop with classic craftsmanship, modern style, and the most photographed wall on Lorain Rd.

The bear on the wall

Walk into Slick Fades and the first thing you meet is the bear: an airbrushed teddy in a backwards teal snapback and gold aviators, clippers in one paw, gold shears in the other, painted floor-to-ceiling on our charcoal wall. We didn't hang stock art, we put our whole personality on the wall.

That's the shop in one image: serious about the craft, never too serious about ourselves.

What we're about

Craft first

Fades checked in two mirrors, lineups finished with a razor, and barbers who keep learning. When you step into our shop, classic craftsmanship meets modern style.

Community always

We're more than just a haircut, we're a community. Regulars are known by name, kids grow up in these chairs, and everyone is welcome.

An experience

Vintage-style leather chairs, good conversation, and a vibe like no other. You come in for the cut; you come back for the shop.

Find us on Lorain Rd

We're at 23601 Lorain Rd in North Olmsted, a minute from Great Northern Mall, serving the whole west side, Westlake, Fairview Park, Rocky River and Olmsted Falls.

Follow the daily work on Instagram @slickfadesbarbershop, bold fades, sharp lines, fresh fades daily.

Questions, answered

Everything people ask before their first visit. Anything else, call (440) 328-6368.

Do I need an appointment at Slick Fades?

No, walk-ins are welcome every day we're open. If you want to time it right, call (440) 328-6368 before you head over and we'll tell you the current wait.

What are your hours?

Tuesday through Saturday 11am–7pm, Sunday 11am–5pm, closed Mondays. We're one of the few North Olmsted barbershops open late AND open Sundays.

Where are you located?

23601 Lorain Rd, North Olmsted, OH 44070, about one minute from Great Northern Mall, with easy parking out front.

How much is a haircut?

Pricing is set in the chair by your barber and depends on length and style. Call (440) 328-6368 for today's prices. Bring cash, the shop doesn't take cards, and there's an ATM inside if you come up short.

Do you take cards?

The shop runs on cash, and there's an ATM right inside if you come up short.

Are you good with kids?

Very, Google literally tags us "good for kids." Parents stay chairside, first haircuts get all the patience they need, and the bear mural does half the babysitting. Details on the kids haircuts page.

Do you do eyebrows?

Yes. Eyebrow threading or waxing, plus nose, ear and full face wax. Most guys add it to their cut; it barely adds any time.

What fade styles do you cut?

All of them: skin fades, shadow fades, taper fades, drop fades, burst fades, mid and high fades, plus scissor work for longer styles. Breakdown on the fades page.

I'm coming from Westlake / Rocky River / Fairview Park, is it far?

Not at all. We're 5–12 minutes from most of the west side. See directions from Westlake, Rocky River, Fairview Park and Olmsted Falls.

Still wondering?

The fastest answer is a phone call, or just walk in and ask.
